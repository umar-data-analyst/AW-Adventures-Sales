## 🚀 Overview
Transform raw sales data into **actionable business insights** with a **fully interactive Power BI Dashboard**. Features include **DAX measures and calculated columns**, dynamic **KPIs, predictive insights, drill-throughs, filters, slicers**, and **automation** for real-time reporting.

> *Designed to empower executives and managers to make data-driven decisions with minimal manual effort.*

## Project Purpose and Context

The AW Adventures Sales Dashboard was developed to transform raw transactional and operational sales data into actionable business intelligence. The goal was to provide executives, managers, and analysts with a comprehensive, interactive platform that enables quick decision-making, trend analysis, and predictive insights, all in real time.

Sales organizations often struggle with fragmented data—spreadsheets, multiple reports, and disconnected systems make it hard to track KPIs, monitor trends, and understand customer behavior. This dashboard centralizes all critical sales and customer data, automates reporting, and provides a clear, visual narrative of the business’s performance.

---

## 🔹 Features
- **Executive Dashboard** – High-level KPIs and sales trends  
- **Customer Analytics** – Behavior, segmentation, lifetime value  
- **Product Analytics** – Performance trends and profitability  
- **Interactive Map Dashboard** – Regional sales heatmaps  
- **Drill-Throughs & Filters** – Explore data dynamically  
- **Predictive Insights** – Forecast sales trends  
- **Automated Reporting** – Refreshes & scheduled dashboards
